# Fixora – Smart Service & Repair Management Platform

## Project Overview

Fixora is a Smart Service & Repair Management Platform designed to connect customers with service professionals through a centralized digital platform. It aims to make finding, requesting, booking and managing repair and maintenance services easier and more organized.

Customers will be able to explore available services, submit service requests, receive quotations, confirm bookings and track service progress. Service providers will be able to manage requests, submit quotations and update job statuses, while administrators will supervise platform activities.

## Project Objectives

- Connect customers with service professionals through a centralized platform.
- Organize different repair and maintenance services into categories.
- Allow customers to submit service requests and receive quotations.
- Provide a structured booking and appointment management system.
- Enable customers to track service progress through status updates.
- Allow customers to submit ratings, reviews and complaints.
- Provide administrative facilities for managing users and service providers.

## Main Service Categories

1. **Home Electrical & Plumbing:** Electrical repairs, wiring, plumbing and water-pump repairs.
2. **Home Maintenance:** AC servicing, carpentry, painting and appliance repairs.
3. **Automobile Services:** Vehicle servicing, battery replacement, tyre repairs and engine diagnostics.
4. **Additional Services:** Deep cleaning, pest control, interior maintenance and furniture installation.

## Main User Roles

- **Customer:** Browse services, submit service requests, compare quotations, manage bookings and track service progress.
- **Service Provider:** Manage professional profiles, respond to service requests, submit quotations and update job progress.
- **Administrator:** Manage users, verify service providers, manage service categories, monitor bookings and handle complaints.
